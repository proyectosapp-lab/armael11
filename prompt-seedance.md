# Prompt para Seedance — video promocional de Armá el 11

Seedance responde mejor en inglés y con una escena por prompt. Va un prompt principal de 10–15 segundos y dos alternativos, todos en 9:16 para Reels. El video generado se usa **solo como apertura o cierre** (los primeros 3–5 segundos y el logo); el medio del reel sigue siendo la grabación real de la app, que es lo que hay que mostrar. Un video generado que "muestra la app" inventa una interfaz que no existe y es lo primero que la gente nota.

Lo que no puede aparecer, y está en los negativos: escudos de clubes, camisetas reconocibles, caras de jugadores reales, logos de la Liga, dinero, fichas, cuotas, casinos, celulares con interfaces inventadas.

---

## Prompt principal — "El once en la cabeza" (12 s)

```
Vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, cinematic realism, shallow depth of field, 24 fps.

A man in his thirties rides a city bus at dusk in Buenos Aires, forehead against
the window, unbranded dark jacket. Reflected faintly in the glass, a football
pitch drawn in thin white lines glows on green: eleven small round tokens slide
into a 5-3-2 formation, one token drifts forward, the others adjust. Over the
tokens, fine white numbers tick: "41" becomes "47". The reflection is subtle,
like a thought. Warm sodium streetlights pass outside. He half-smiles.

Cut to: the same pitch diagram, now filling the frame, seen from above at a low
angle, dark green grass with faint stripes, tokens moving in a slow tactical
push, a single white ball rolling into the net. No crowd, no stadium, just the
diagram-like pitch under a night sky. The counter "6.000" fades in, white, thin.

Final 2 seconds: clean white rounded tile on a green grass gradient, the number
"11" in green inside the tile, centered, still. Minimal, poster-like.

Mood: quiet confidence, nocturnal, tactile. Color palette: pitch green #177A40,
near-black #0D1117, white. No text other than the numbers. Natural motion, no
glitch effects, no lens flares.
```

**Negativo:**
```
club crests, team badges, recognizable football kits, real players' faces,
league logos, stadium branding, sponsor boards, money, coins, casino chips,
betting odds, slot machines, smartphone with an app interface, fake UI, text
overlays, subtitles, watermark, cartoon style, anime, extra fingers, distorted
hands, flicker, fast cuts.
```

## Alternativo A — "La discusión" (10 s)

```
Vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, handheld documentary feel, warm interior light.

Two friends at a small bar table in Argentina, late night, a half-empty bottle
and two glasses, paper napkin between them. One draws a football formation on
the napkin with a pen: eleven dots, then crosses one out and moves it forward.
The other shakes his head, takes the pen, moves it back. Close on the napkin:
the pen dots become glowing green tokens on a pitch diagram that lifts off the
paper and hovers, tokens sliding into formation, a small white number "44"
appearing beside it. Both look at it, then at each other.

Last 2 seconds: white rounded tile with a green "11" on a green grass gradient,
still, centered.

No club colors on clothing (plain shirts, muted tones). Palette: warm amber
bar light, pitch green #177A40, white. No text, no logos.
```

Mismo negativo que el principal.

## Alternativo B — "6.000 veces" (8 s, para historia o cierre)

```
Vertical 9:16, 8 seconds, top-down view, minimal, dark.

A single football pitch diagram on dark green, thin white lines. Eleven red
tokens and eleven blue tokens (plain colors, no emblems). The same match plays
out in fast forward again and again, each run a faint ghost layered over the
last, hundreds of translucent trails accumulating into a heat map of movement,
the ball's paths drawing bright white threads toward both goals. A counter at
the top climbs quickly from 1 to 6.000 in thin white digits, then stops.

Everything freezes. The trails fade. Only the final formation remains, sharp.

Last 2 seconds: white rounded tile with a green "11", centered on a grass
gradient. Palette: #177A40 green, #0D1117, white, one red and one blue accent.
No text other than the counter.
```

Negativo: el mismo, más `stadium, crowd, real footage`.

---

## Cómo se usa

1. Generá el principal en 9:16 y dos o tres variantes de semilla; elegí la que tenga las manos y las caras más limpias (es donde Seedance falla más).
2. Cortá los últimos 2 segundos (la loseta): esa parte va reemplazada por el logo real, `foto-perfil-a.png`, en el editor, para que sea idéntico al de la cuenta. El generado es solo referencia de composición.
3. El video generado abre el reel; a los 3–4 segundos se corta a la grabación real de la app. La voz entra sobre el generado: "¿Seguís tirando un dado?"
4. Si Seedance mete un escudo, una camiseta de club o un celular con pantalla inventada, se descarta la toma. No se recorta ni se tapa: se vuelve a generar.

## Reel entero generado — "¿Seguís tirando un dado?" (para comparar con el grabado)

La misma estructura del reel 1, pero generada. Seedance no puede mostrar la app real, así que en vez de la pantalla del teléfono usa **el mismo diagrama de cancha con fichas** que en los otros prompts; los subtítulos y la voz los ponés vos en CapCut, por eso el video va sin texto salvo los números. Son dos clips de 9 segundos (Seedance rinde mejor en clips cortos que en uno de 18) que se pegan en el editor.

**Clip 1 (0–9 s)**
```
Vertical 9:16, 9 seconds, cinematic realism, 24 fps, shallow depth of field.

Extreme close-up: a single white six-sided die tumbles across a worn wooden
table in warm window light, slows, wobbles, and stops. Dust motes in the light.
Hold on the die for a beat.

Then the tabletop grain dissolves into dark green grass with faint stripes seen
from directly above: a football pitch drawn in thin white lines. Eleven small
red round tokens and eleven blue ones slide into formation. The match plays out
in fast forward, again and again, each run a translucent ghost layered over the
last, hundreds of trails accumulating. At the top, thin white digits count up
quickly and stop at "6.000".

Palette: warm wood and amber light, then pitch green #177A40, near-black
#0D1117, white. Natural motion, no glitch effects. No text except the counter.
```

**Clip 2 (9–18 s)**
```
Vertical 9:16, 9 seconds, top-down view, cinematic realism, 24 fps.

The same football pitch diagram on dark green grass, thin white lines. The red
tokens hold a deep, compact defensive line. Slowly, as if pulled by a hand off
screen, the whole red back line slides forward twenty meters in unison, and the
blue tokens react, spreading. A pair of thin white numbers beside the pitch
change from "41" to "47" as the line moves. A single white ball rolls into the
far net.

Then the tokens snap back to their starting positions and the exact same short
sequence replays once, identical, frame for frame: same paths, same ball, same
"47". Everything freezes on the final formation, sharp.

Last 2 seconds: a clean white rounded tile with a green "11" inside, centered
on a green grass gradient, still, poster-like.

Palette: pitch green #177A40, near-black #0D1117, white, one red and one blue
accent. No text other than the two numbers. No crowd, no stadium.
```

**Negativo para los dos:**
```
club crests, team badges, recognizable football kits, real players' faces,
league logos, stadium, crowd, sponsor boards, money, coins, casino chips, more
than one die, dice with dollar signs, roulette, slot machines, betting odds,
smartphone, app interface, fake UI, text overlays, subtitles, watermark,
cartoon style, anime, distorted hands, flicker, fast cuts.
```

**Para comparar limpio.** El grabado y el generado no compiten en igualdad: el grabado muestra la app de verdad y el generado no puede. Lo que sí vale comparar es la **apertura**: el dado filmado por vos contra el dado generado, con el mismo resto del reel. Si el generado gana en la apertura, se usa esa toma y el resto sigue siendo la grabación real; un reel entero generado sirve como pieza de marca (historia, portada de destacada), no como demostración de lo que hace la app.
